Darbot Coder – Brand Kit v1
=================================
Primary look: neon terminal-green (#00FF66) on deep black with soft glow.
Use on dark backgrounds. Maintain clear space (~8% of canvas).

Folders:
- master/: 4096, 2048, 1024
- transparent/: 4096 transparent (dark-only)
- platforms/ios, android, web, macos, windows, emoji, social

Usage tips:
- Prefer dark backgrounds for the neon glow effect.
- For light backgrounds, place the mark on a black/dark tile.
- Avoid recoloring the neon green and avoid drop shadows (glow already applied).
- Keep the icon square aspect for app + favicon usage.
